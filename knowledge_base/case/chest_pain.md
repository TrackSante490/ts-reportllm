Ask about:
- Onset and duration
- Location and radiation (arm, jaw, back)
- Character (pressure, sharp, burning)
- Severity (0–10)
- Triggers (exertion, stress)
- Relief (rest)
- Associated symptoms:
  - Shortness of breath
  - Nausea
  - Sweating

Red flags:
- Severe pressure-like pain
- Pain with exertion
- Fainting
